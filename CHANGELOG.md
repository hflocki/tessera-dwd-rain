# Changelog

## 1.1.3
- On a small 1 x 1, one line of words and a smaller circle, so nothing lies under the tile's edge.

## 1.1.2
- The icon sits inside its circle as one part, and an idle circle is lighter, so the grey icon reads in the light look too.
- Amber and yellow words and icons take the ink on the light look, where they were too faint to read; the dark look keeps its colours.

## 1.1.1
- Opacities are written only when they change, so the tile is not redrawn every second.

## 1.1.0
- The tile takes any size from 1 x 1 to 6 x 3: on one column it shows the circle and the sentence under it.

## 1.0.0
- First version: when it rains in the next two hours and for how long, from a DWD precipitation forecast sensor, with
  the forecast as bars.
